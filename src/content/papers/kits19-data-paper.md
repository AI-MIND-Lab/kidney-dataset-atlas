The data descriptor for KiTS19. It is where the catalog's account of the cohort comes from:
nephrectomy patients treated at the University of Minnesota between 2010 and mid-2018,
identified by retrospective chart review, under review board study 1611M00821.

It is recorded as a **descriptor**: it describes the data rather than evaluating methods on
it, so it is not evidence that anyone has used the dataset for computer vision.
