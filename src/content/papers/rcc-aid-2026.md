The paper describes how 1,915 TCGA CT series were screened down to 129 annotated scans, how
students corrected model-generated masks with radiologist review of uncertain cases, and the
resulting cohort. It reports no model of its own; its example use case points to a separate
lesion-classification study by Häntze et al. (2026), which is not catalogued here.

An earlier medRxiv preprint, "Accessible and Reproducible Renal Cell Carcinoma Research Through
Open-Sourcing Data and Annotations", is still the citation given on the Zenodo record and in the
code repository.
