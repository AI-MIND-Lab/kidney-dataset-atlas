The model is developed on 308 patients from one hospital (258 training, 50 test) and compared
with six radiologists. For external validation the authors collect 184 multi-phase CT scans from
The Cancer Imaging Archive (163 clear cell, 14 papillary, 7 chromophobe), citing the TCGA-KIRC,
TCGA-KIRP and TCGA-KICH collections; oncocytoma and angiomyolipoma have no cases there, so the
external test covers three of the five classes.
