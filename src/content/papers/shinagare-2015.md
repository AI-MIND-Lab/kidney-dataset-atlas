The study behind TCGA-KIRC-Radiogenomics, which The Cancer Imaging Archive names as the best source
of further information about that table. The TCGA Renal Cell Carcinoma Imaging Research Group
scored size, margin, composition, necrosis, growth pattern and calcification for 103 tumours and
related them to VHL, PBRM1, BAP1, SETD2, KDM5C and MUC4 mutations.

In the authors' words, "BAP1 mutation was associated with ill-defined tumor margins and presence of
calcification; MUC4 mutation was associated with exophytic growth", but "none of these associations
remained significant after adjusting for multiple testing".

It is recorded as the release's **original** publication. It evaluates no image model, so it is not
evidence of computer-vision use. The paper is not CC BY (Crossref lists only Springer's text and
data mining licence; the PMC copy is an author manuscript), so none of its figures are reproduced
here.
