The challenge report for the HuBMAP kidney competition. It documents the released kidney
slides and their glomerulus annotations, and evaluates the algorithms that 1,200 teams
submitted.

It is recorded as a **benchmark**: it evaluates methods on the organisers' own data. That is
real computer-vision use, but it is not evidence that anyone outside the competition has
adopted the dataset.

Individual competitor models are not catalogued here. The author list is shortened.
