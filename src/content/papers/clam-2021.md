CLAM learns which regions of a slide matter for a slide-level label, without any region
annotations. The renal task uses 884 TCGA diagnostic slides from the three kidney cancer
projects, cross-validated ten times with an 80/10/10 split by case, and the trained models are
also tested on an independent set of 135 renal slides from Brigham and Women's Hospital.

The figures recorded here come from the arXiv version of the paper (v2, May 2020), which was
the version read; the journal version may report them differently.
