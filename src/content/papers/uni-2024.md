For renal subtyping, UNI's authors use 922 TCGA diagnostic FFPE slides after quality control
(519 clear cell, 294 papillary, 109 chromophobe) split 80:10:10 by label, and test externally on
468 slides from Dartmouth-Hitchcock Medical Center combined with 404 clear cell slides from
CPTAC. The subtype-level figures are in the paper's supplementary tables, which were not read
for this record, so no result is recorded here.
