NHANES combines interviews, physical examinations and laboratory testing in a survey
designed to represent the US population. For kidney research its value is in the laboratory
files: serum creatinine and urea nitrogen, urine albumin and creatinine, and the urine
albumin-to-creatinine ratio, measured on thousands of participants in each cycle.

This record covers the kidney-related laboratory files of the August 2021–August 2023
cycle. These are survey participants, not a clinical cohort, and the survey is
cross-sectional: each cycle samples people anew rather than following the same people over
time.

Analyses of these files need the survey design variables and weights, which live in the
demographics file of the same cycle and are joined by the participant sequence number.
