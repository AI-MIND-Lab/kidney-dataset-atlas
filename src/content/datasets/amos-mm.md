AMOS-MM is the dataset of the AMOS-MM challenge at MICCAI 2024, organised by a team from the University of Hong Kong
and Stanford University as an extension of AMOS22, an earlier organ-segmentation dataset (design document, pages 2
and 5). Each case is one CT scan with its radiology report, split into findings and impression for the chest,
abdomen and pelvis, and for most cases a set of multiple-choice questions.

The reports are the hospitals' own. They were written in Chinese, translated into English by three junior
radiologists and corrected by two senior radiologists (page 13). The questions, their answers and a short reasoning
were generated by GPT-4 from the reports and then reviewed by a senior radiologist and a clinical physician
(page 26).

The kidneys come up often but are not the focus. The reports describe every organ in the scan, and no kidney masks or
labels are released. A keyword search of the impressions finds a kidney cyst in about 500 of the 1,687 cases and a
kidney stone in about 300; the exact numbers depend on the search rule.

Two things are easy to misread. The release holds 1,687 cases, not the 2,000 or more the pages announce: the
400 test cases are not public (see the quality findings). And 228 of the CT files are AMOS22 files, which have
organ masks in AMOS22. 152 of them are also AbdomenAtlas 3.0 and DeepTumorVQA scans, with AbdomenAtlas's organ and
lesion masks as well, so their counts must not be added.
