The papillary renal cell carcinoma project of The Cancer Genome Atlas. As with the other TCGA
renal projects, **the GDC release covers the open-access tier only**: clinical and biospecimen
records and whole-slide images. Low-level sequencing and germline data sit in the
controlled-access tier and need dbGaP authorisation.

KIRP is one of three TCGA renal projects catalogued here, alongside clear cell (KIRC) and
chromophobe (KICH). They are separate cohorts that share a distribution platform, and one
study using all three does not make them one dataset.

**Radiology is a second release.** The Cancer Imaging Archive holds imaging for 33 of the 291
cases (23 with CT), identified by the same TCGA barcodes, so it joins to the slides and
clinical records here. The archive distributes images only; RCC-AID, catalogued as its own
dataset, adds kidney, tumour and cyst masks for 26 of these CT scans.
