Kidney histology with glomeruli outlined — the functional tissue unit whose count and
condition matter in most kidney pathology. That makes this the closest thing in the catalog to
a kidney-specific pathology annotation set, as opposed to slides with case-level labels.

The annotations were not drawn from scratch: a pipeline produced initial segmentations, which
subject matter experts then inspected and corrected in QuPath. That is stronger than a purely
automatic set and weaker than independent manual delineation, and it is worth knowing before
treating these outlines as a gold standard.
