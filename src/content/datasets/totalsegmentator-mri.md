The MRI companion to TotalSegmentator CT, and a separate collection rather than another
version of it: different images, different structures, its own archive and its own DOI.

For kidney work its value is that MRI kidney outlines are scarce. As with the CT collection,
nothing here is selected for kidney disease, and a kidney label is an anatomical outline.

Sequences vary, which is the point of the collection, but it also means a model trained here
has seen a mixture rather than one protocol.
