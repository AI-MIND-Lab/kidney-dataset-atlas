# Kidney Dataset Atlas

A curated inventory of public kidney research datasets supporting research in segmentation, imaging biomarkers, classification, and multimodal artificial intelligence.

The atlas documents dataset sources, imaging modalities, annotations, clinical variables, access requirements, and data dictionaries. Dataset files remain with their original providers unless redistribution is explicitly permitted.

## Status

This resource is under development. Entries are being reviewed for accuracy, completeness, and dataset overlap.
